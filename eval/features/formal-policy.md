# Data processing policy

This policy describes how Contoso Ltd. ("Contoso", "we") processes personal data in connection with the Contoso Sync service ("the Service").

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to all customers who use the Service under a commercial agreement. It does not apply to third-party services that customers connect to the Service.

## 2. Categories of data

We process the following categories of personal data:

1. Account data, such as name, e-mail address and organization.
2. Usage data, such as sign-in times and the number of synced files.
3. Customer content, which we process solely to provide the Service.

## 3. Retention

Customer content is deleted within 30 days after the end of the subscription, unless the customer requests earlier deletion in writing.

## 4. Subprocessors

We engage subprocessors only under written agreements that impose obligations equivalent to those in this policy. A current list is available upon request.

## 5. Rights of data subjects

Data subjects may exercise their rights of access, rectification, erasure and objection by contacting the customer that is the controller. We will assist the customer within a reasonable period.
