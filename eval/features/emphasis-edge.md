# Formatting edge cases

This sentence has **bold text with *nested italics* inside** and ends normally.

Intraword emphasis: un**believ**able and snake_case_names_stay and file_name.txt stays.

Mixed ***bold and italic*** text, ~~struck-through words~~ and `inline code` in one line.

**Bold at the start** of a sentence and *italic at the end*.

A sentence with a **bold [link](https://example.com)** inside.

Multiple spans: **Open**, **Edit** and **Save** are on the **File** menu.

Underscore emphasis: _this is emphasized_ and __this is strong__.

A literal asterisk \* and a literal underscore \_ must stay escaped.

Adjacent spans: **one**_two_ and `code`**bold**.

An emphasized file name: *settings.json* and a bold path: **C:\Temp\sync**.
