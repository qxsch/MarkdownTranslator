# Pricing

The Team plan costs $5 per user and month, and the Enterprise plan costs $12 per user and month.

Storage add-ons cost $2 for 100 GB and $15 for 1 TB.

If you pay yearly, you save $10 per user, so the Team plan costs $50 and not $60.

Shell users can read the price from the API with `curl -s $API/prices | jq .team` and store it in $PRICE for later.

Education customers get a 50% discount: $2.50 instead of $5.

Prices in other currencies are shown at checkout. Taxes such as VAT are added where required.

Compare: between $5 and $12 per seat, depending on the plan.
