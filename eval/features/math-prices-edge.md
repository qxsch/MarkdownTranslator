# Billing examples

Most customers pay between $4 and $12 per user each month. The Team plan is $5/month per user, and the Enterprise plan is $1,299.99 per year for up to 20 users.

A price range such as $5-$10 is shown on the pricing page. Discounts of US$20 apply to yearly contracts, and the setup fee of $0 is waived for nonprofits.

If you downgrade, you pay $3 less per user and save $36 per year.

Scripts can read your plan with `ctsync billing show` and store the total in $TOTAL, or export it to $HOME/billing.csv for accounting.

Invoices list amounts like $49.00 and $149.00 separately for each workspace.
