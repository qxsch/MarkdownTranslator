# Sharing roles

<RoleCard role="viewer" title="Viewer" description="Can open and download files." icon="eye" />

<RoleCard role="editor" title="Editor" description="Can change, add and delete files." icon="pencil" />

<Admonition type="tip" title="Least privilege">
  Give people the **lowest** role that lets them do their work.
</Admonition>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the folder">Right-click the folder in File Explorer.</Step>
  <Step title="Choose a role">Pick *Viewer* or *Editor* from the list.</Step>
  <Step title="Send the invitation">Select **Send**.</Step>
</Steps>
