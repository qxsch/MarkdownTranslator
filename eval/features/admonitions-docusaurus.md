# Configure retention

:::note
Retention applies to deleted files and to old versions.
:::

:::tip Save space
Set a short retention for folders with large media files.
:::

:::info[Retention and legal hold]
Files on **legal hold** are kept even when the retention period ends.
:::

:::warning

The retention setting affects **all** members of the share.

:::

:::danger Irreversible
Files that are deleted by the retention policy can't be restored.
:::

## Choose a period

Most teams keep versions for 30 days. Regulated industries often need 7 years.
