# Unicode and typography

Smart quotes: “Keep both” and ‘Keep newest’ are the two options.

Dashes and ellipses: 2020–2026 and “Wait…” are typographic characters.

Non-breaking spaces: 10 GB and Contoso Sync 3.2 contain U+00A0.

Emoji: ✅ Synced, ⚠️ Needs attention, ❌ Failed, 🔒 Locked.

Arrows and symbols: Settings → Network → Proxy, © Contoso, ™, ±5%.

Accented names stay as they are: Zoë Müller, François Nguyễn and Łukasz Żółć work on the team.

Right-to-left text in a sentence: the Arabic word مرحبا means hello.

Zero-width joiner sequences: 👩‍💻 and 🏳️‍🌈.

Math symbols in prose: ≤ 5 minutes, ≥ 99.9% availability, × 3 faster.
