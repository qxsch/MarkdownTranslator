# Share a folder

Right-click the folder and select **Share**. Enter the names or e-mail addresses of the people you want to share with.

## Permissions

- **Can view**: People can open and download files.
- **Can edit**: People can also change, add and delete files.
- **Owner**: People can also manage sharing.

## External users

External users get an e-mail with a link. They must verify their e-mail address with a one-time code.
