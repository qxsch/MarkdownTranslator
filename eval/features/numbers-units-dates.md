|---|---|

The service processes 3.5 million files per hour at peak. The last maintenance window was on 14 August 2026 from 01:00 to 03:30 UTC.

Retention can be set between 1 and 3,650 days. The default of 93 days matches the recycle bin.

Version 3.2 reduced the average sync delay from 2.4 s to 0.8 s, which is 66.7% faster.

Plans start at 4.50 EUR per user per month, billed annually (54 EUR per year).
